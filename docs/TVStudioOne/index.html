TV Studio One

TV and Radio Filter

Find what you actually want to watch — on UK Freeview and across Europe — in a few taps.

Download on the App Store

What it does

A schedule, distilled.

Every UK Freeview channel — or the schedule for any of 26 European countries — plus the major subscription services, sorted into six focused tabs. No more scrolling through 200 channels to find tonight's film.

Now across Europe

Pick your country and get its TV schedule — 26 European countries alongside the UK, from Austria to Sweden. Same six tabs, same smart filtering, wherever you are.

Speaks your language

The whole app is localised into 21 languages — including German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese. Choose your language in Settings, or let it follow your device.

Six tabs

Sports, Movies, Faith, Talk, More and Favourites. Each pill shows only what fits, with its own colour, its own header, and its own logic.

Smart classification

Fast heuristic detection on every programme, with an optional AI enhancement — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT) or Google (Gemini) — when you add an API key. Turn it on and the tricky cases get found: films hiding on general channels, animated movies the guide only labels "animation".

Reminders, on time

Tap the bell to be notified ten minutes before a programme begins. Native iOS notifications — no account, no servers, no sign-in.

Genre filters

The More tab respects thirteen content genres — Documentary, Drama, Comedy, Game Shows, Reality, News, Lifestyle, Animation, Music, Sci-Fi, Horror, True Crime and Western. Toggle the ones you want, hide the ones you don't.

Your region

Set your UK region and TV Studio One hides the ITV and BBC One variants that aren't yours — no more duplicate Anglia, Granada and London cluttering the guide.

Yours, not theirs

Subscribe to Sky Sports, Discovery+, TNT? Toggle them on. Each tab now shows your subscription channels alongside Freeview.

Live scorecards

Open any live sporting event for an on-demand scorecard — current score for team sports, ranked leaderboard for Formula 1 and golf. Add your own sports-data API key for a faster live feed.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The schedule loads anonymously from community EPG sources. Your favourites, reminders and preferences stay on your device, syncing across your own devices via iCloud if you choose.

Built for the way you watch.

TV Studio One was designed for households tired of fighting their TV guide. Open the app, tap the genre you're in the mood for, see what's on now and what's coming. That's it.
