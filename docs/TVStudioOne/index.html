Studio One

TV and Radio Filter — find what you actually want to watch on UK Freeview, in a few taps.

What it does

A schedule, distilled.

Every UK Freeview channel, plus the major subscription services, sorted into six focused tabs. No more scrolling through 200 channels to find tonight's film.

Six tabs

Sports, Movies, Faith, Talk, Custom and Favourites. Each pill shows only what fits, with its own colour, its own header, and its own logic.

Smart classification

Heuristic detection on every programme, refined by your choice of AI — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT) or Google (Gemini) — if you add an API key. Get it wrong? Reclassify in one tap — your choice is remembered forever.

Reminders, on time

Tap the bell to be notified ten minutes before a programme begins. Native iOS notifications — no account, no servers, no sign-in.

Custom filters

The Custom tab respects twelve content genres — Documentary, Drama, Comedy, GameShow, Reality, News, Lifestyle, Animation, Music, Sci-Fi, Horror, True Crime. Toggle the ones you want, hide the ones you don't.

Yours, not theirs

Subscribe to Sky Sports, Discovery+, TNT? Toggle them on. Each tab now shows your subscription channels alongside Freeview.

Live scorecards

Open any live sporting event for an on-demand scorecard — current score for team sports, ranked leaderboard for Formula 1 and golf. Add your own sports-data API key for a faster live feed.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The schedule loads anonymously from community EPG sources. Your favourites, reminders and preferences stay on your device, syncing across your own devices via iCloud if you choose.

Built for the way you watch.

Studio One was designed for households tired of fighting their TV guide. Open the app, tap the genre you're in the mood for, see what's on now and what's coming. That's it.
