Support

Straight answers about how TV Studio One works.

Getting Started

Which devices does TV Studio One run on?

TV Studio One requires iOS 17 or later. It runs on iPhone and iPad. A Mac Catalyst build is on the roadmap.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. There is no account, no sign-in, and no email registration. Open the app and the UK Freeview schedule loads automatically.

Why are some currently airing programmes missing?

TV Studio One only shows what its broadcaster reports in the EPG. If a programme isn't listed, it's almost always because the broadcaster hasn't published it. Pull down on the schedule to force a refresh.

Using the Tabs

What's the difference between the six tabs?

Sports: live sporting events and sports-radio output. Movies: feature films and made-for-TV movies. Faith: religious channels, worship, and faith-themed programming. Talk: chat shows, panel shows, sports-talk and rolling news. More: everything else — dramas, documentaries, comedy, reality, and radio — sortable by content genre in Settings. Favourites: heart a title and every future airing appears here — across days, channels and repeats.

A programme is in the wrong tab. What can I do?

Classification is automatic — the app sorts each programme from its channel, the TV guide's category tags, and title keywords. Occasionally the guide's own data is unusual and a programme lands in the wrong place. Two things help: pull down on the schedule to fetch the latest guide data, and turn on the optional AI enhancement (see below), which corrects many cases the raw guide data gets wrong — such as a film hiding on a general entertainment channel. Classifications are curated and improve with each app update; if something is consistently misplaced, email us and we'll fix it at the source.

Why do I see several versions of ITV or BBC One?

UK Freeview carries regional variants of ITV and BBC One (ITV Anglia, ITV Granada, ITV London, and so on). By default TV Studio One shows them all. Open Settings → My Region and choose your region, and the app hides the variants that aren't local to you — leaving just your own ITV and BBC One. Choose "All regions" to show every variant again.

How do I get my subscription channels (Sky, TNT, etc.) to appear?

Open Settings (the gear icon in the top right). Enable the services you subscribe to under Subscription Services. Their channels will then appear in every tab alongside Freeview channels.

Reminders

How do reminders work?

Tap the bell icon on any programme that hasn't started yet. TV Studio One schedules a local iOS notification ten minutes before the programme begins. Tap the bell again to cancel.

Do my favourites remind me automatically?

Yes. When you heart a title, TV Studio One automatically sets a reminder for every upcoming airing of it — you don't need to tap the bell on each one, and it keeps working across app restarts and as new listings arrive. Notifications need permission (see below), and because iOS limits how many can be scheduled at once, the soonest airings are prioritised.

Why doesn't the bell appear on some programmes?

The bell only shows when there's still time to set a useful reminder — i.e. when the programme is more than ten minutes from starting. Already-aired programmes and imminent ones don't show one.

I missed a notification. What happened?

Check Settings → Notifications → TV Studio One on your device. The first time you set a reminder, iOS asks for permission; if you decline, no notifications can be delivered until you turn permission back on in iOS Settings.

AI Classification

Do I need an AI API key to use the app?

No. TV Studio One works fully without one — it falls back to fast built-in heuristics that handle the large majority of the schedule. The AI is an optional classification enhancement that sharpens the tricky cases, not a requirement.

How do I turn on the AI classification enhancement?

Open Settings → AI Classification, add a provider key, then switch on "Enrich full schedule with AI". The AI then reads each programme's title and categories and assigns a genre, noticeably improving which tab programmes land in — for example catching a feature film the guide shows on a general channel, or an animated movie the guide labels only as "animation". Each title is sent once and cached, so it's cheap to run.

Which AI providers are supported?

You can add your own key for Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), or Google (Gemini). All three offer pay-as-you-go pricing. Get keys at console.anthropic.com, platform.openai.com, or aistudio.google.com.

How much does AI classification cost in practice?

Approximately 1–5 pence per day. Each programme title is sent only once and cached permanently on your device, so cost is bounded by the size of the daily schedule and shrinks over time as the cache fills.

How do I remove my API key?

Go to Settings and clear your key there. It is removed from the iOS Keychain immediately. You can also revoke it at the source in your provider's console.

Live Sports Scores

How do I see a live scorecard?

Open a live sporting event and tap Live scorecard. Team sports show the current score; ranked sports like Formula 1 and golf show a leaderboard. Pull Refresh for the latest. The free version reads scores via AI web search — accurate to when you open or refresh.

What is Premium Live Data?

Optional. Add your own key for a sports-data provider (Settings → Premium Live Data) for a faster, real live feed. API-Sports has a free tier that covers live football and Formula 1. Use "Test connection" to confirm your key works. Any sport not yet served by a premium provider falls back to the free AI scorecard automatically.

Does the app show betting odds?

No. TV Studio One only ever shows scores, results and leaderboards — never betting odds or gambling information, by design.

Privacy & Data

What data do you collect?

None. We have no servers, accounts, analytics or tracking. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what leaves your device when you use the optional AI and live-score features.

Where does the schedule come from?

The public UK Freeview schedule from community sources — the DP247 Freeview-EPG project and epgshare01 — fetched anonymously with no identifier or location data.
