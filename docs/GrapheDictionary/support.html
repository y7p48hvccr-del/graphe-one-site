Support

Graphē Dictionary for Mac, iPhone and iPad

Email support@graphe.one. Please say which device you use (Mac, iPhone or iPad), the version of macOS or iOS, and the word or screen involved. We reply to every message.

Common questions

The Examples or Quotations switches show nothing.

The bundled Essential dictionary has no examples or quotations. They come with the Maximum size, which you can download in More ▸ Settings. If you have it already, make sure the switches are filled (on), not outlined.

The Wikipedia tab is blank.

It needs an internet connection. Wikipedia may take a few seconds to load a page. If a word has no Wikipedia article of its own, you see Wikipedia's search page instead.

Looking up a highlighted word on Mac.

Use Services ▸ Look Up in Graphē Dictionary in any app, or click the Graphē Dictionary Dock icon after highlighting a word. The Dock-click needs the Accessibility permission described in Help.

Ontology and Wikipedia are locked.

Your seven-day trial has ended. Open More ▸ Settings and choose Unlock Everything. If you have already bought it on another device, choose Restore Purchases.

The Webster's switch has hidden all my results.

With Webster's switched on, only the Webster's 1913 Dictionary (modernised) definitions are shown. Switch it off for everything else.

How do I download a bigger dictionary?

See Settings in Help.
