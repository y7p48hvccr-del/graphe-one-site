Graphē Dictionary Privacy Statement

Effective 12 November 2026

The short version: Graphē Dictionary collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The words you look up are handled on your device and are never sent to us. Studio Graphē One Ltd. operates no servers that receive your data.

What stays on your device

  • The dictionary itself — the bundled dictionary and any larger one you download are files on your device. Searching and reading use only those files.
  • Your preferences — text size, which switches are on (Examples, Quotations, Webster's), spelling choice, the topic you filtered by, and any categories you have hidden.
  • Your session — the list of words you have visited this session, used by the Ontology journey. It is not saved when the app closes.
  • A folder bookmark — if you choose a folder to load dictionary files from (for example one in iCloud Drive), the app remembers where it is so it can reopen it. The app reads only the dictionary files you point it at.

None of this is sent to Studio Graphē One or to anyone else.

When the app connects to the internet

The dictionary and thesaurus work without a connection. The app uses the internet in two situations only:

  • Downloading a larger dictionary. In Settings, you can download a bigger size. The files are fetched from a public storage bucket on Cloudflare R2. As with any web download, the host can see your IP address and which file you asked for. Studio Graphē One does not receive, keep or analyse these requests, and the app sends nothing else.
  • The Wikipedia tab. When you choose Wikipedia, the app asks Wikipedia for the article matching the word you are reading, and shows the page from en.wikipedia.org inside the app. The word, your IP address and ordinary web-browsing information go to Wikipedia, which is run by the Wikimedia Foundation and is governed by the Wikimedia Privacy Policy. Links to other websites open in your normal browser.

Purchases

Unlock Everything is an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. Payment details never reach Studio Graphē One, and we receive no personal information about you or your purchase. The app asks Apple whether this Apple Account has already bought the unlock, and uses the date you first downloaded the app, which Apple provides, to work out the seven-day trial.

Looking up text from other apps

  • iPhone and iPad. When you open the app, it can read short copied text from the clipboard and put it in the search box. iOS asks your permission, and you can change that under Settings ▸ Graphē Dictionary ▸ Paste from Other Apps. The text is used only to fill the search box and goes nowhere else.
  • Mac. The Services menu item "Look Up in Graphē Dictionary" receives the text you selected. Clicking the Dock icon can also look up the word highlighted in the app you were using; this needs your permission under System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Accessibility, and you can withdraw it at any time. The selected text is used only to look up that word and is not stored. If the Wikipedia tab is showing, that word is sent to Wikipedia as described above.

What Graphē Dictionary does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No accounts, and no sale, sharing or transfer of personal data — we hold none to share.
  • No third-party software development kits that collect data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Support correspondence

If you email support@graphe.one, we use your message and address only to reply to you. We do not add you to lists or share your correspondence.

Changes

If this statement ever changes, the current version will always be at this address, with its effective date above.
