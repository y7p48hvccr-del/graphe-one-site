What it does

  • A very large dictionary — over 1.4 million headwords and 2.1 million senses in the largest size, from Open English WordNet, Wiktionary, Webster's 1913 Dictionary (modernised) and more. Choose the size that suits your device; the bundled one works offline.
  • Dictionary — definitions, examples and quotations from Open English WordNet, Wiktionary and Webster's 1913 Dictionary (modernised), with computing terms from FOLDOC.
  • Dictionaries in 20 languages — optional downloads of Wiktionary in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Polish, Czech, Greek, Indonesian, Malay, Vietnamese, Thai and Kurdish, plus Simple English, each with its definitions written in that language. They sit beside English rather than replacing it, and a button beside the search box switches between them.
  • Thesaurus — the closest words in meaning first, then words by meaning, broader and narrower terms, and opposites.
  • Wikipedia — the article for the word you are reading, with the page furniture stripped away. It opens in the language of the dictionary you are using, or in any of Wikipedia's 300-plus languages you choose: look up “lighthouse” and read Spanish Wikipedia's “Faro”.
  • My Dictionary — add your own words and meanings in any language, such as jargon or names. They turn up first in searches beside everything else and sync between your devices through your own iCloud account.
  • In your language — the app itself is in English and 19 other languages: Čeština, Deutsch, Ελληνικά, Español, Français, Bahasa Indonesia, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어, Kurdî, Bahasa Melayu, Nederlands, Polski, Português, Русский, ไทย, Türkçe, Tiếng Việt and 简体中文. Choose one in Settings.
  • British or American — English definitions in British spelling (Oxford spelling, with -ize) or American, whichever you prefer.
  • Ontology — retrace the words you have visited, or explore a word's neighbours as a map.
  • Yours to size — the bundled dictionary works offline; larger ones are an optional download.

Graphē Dictionary comes with Essential. The larger sizes are optional downloads, from inside the app (More ▸ Settings). You can also keep the dictionary files in a folder of your own, such as iCloud Drive or an external drive connected to your iPhone or iPad, and copy a size from there. The app keeps its own copy on the device, so the drive can be disconnected afterwards, and the device still needs enough free space for it. Headwords include phrases and word forms.

Dictionaries in other languages

With Unlock Everything you can also download Wiktionary in 20 languages, each with definitions, examples, word origins and translations written in that language. Download as many as you like from More ▸ Settings ▸ Other languages; each sits beside English, and the flag button beside the search box chooses which dictionary to look words up in, whatever language the app itself is in.
