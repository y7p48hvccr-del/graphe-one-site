Help

Graphē Dictionary for Mac, iPhone and iPad

Looking up a word

Type in the search box at the top of the word list. Matching headwords come first, then words whose meaning contains what you typed. On the Mac, press ⌘F to jump to the search box. Choose a word from the list to read it. On iPhone, choosing a word opens it full screen; swipe from the left edge to go back to the list.

To look up a word inside an article, double-tap it (or double-click on a Mac).

Dictionary, Thesaurus and Wikipedia

The three pills at the top of the article switch how the chosen word is shown:

  • Dictionary — numbered senses with their source, pronunciation, related words, and optional examples, quotations and translations.
  • Thesaurus — Closest in meaning first (larger words are nearer), then groups by meaning, broader and narrower terms, related words and opposites. Tap any word to open it.
  • Wikipedia — the Wikipedia article for the word, inside the app. It needs an internet connection.

Examples, Quotations and Webster's

On the Dictionary page, three small switches sit beside the pills. A filled switch is on; an outlined one is off.

  • Examples and Quotations show or hide those parts of each sense. They are included with the Maximum dictionary.
  • Webster's — when on, only Webster's 1913 definitions are shown, across search, lists and pages. When off (the usual setting), Webster's definitions are hidden and everything else shows.

Text size

Tap the Aa button beside the pills and drag the slider. It runs from small but readable to large. On the Mac you can also use ⌘+, ⌘− and ⌘0 (normal size). Text size applies to the article, not the word list.

Topics

Tap the tag button to search within one subject, such as Computing. Type in the box to find a topic, and choose All Topics to search everything again. To hide a topic you never use from the list, slide it to the left (on a Mac you can also right-click it) and choose Hide. Hiding a topic only removes it from the list; its words remain in the dictionary. Hidden topics can be brought back from Dictionary Data.

Ontology: your journey and the word map

Tap the slate-blue Ontology button for a card with two views:

  • Journey — the words you have visited this session, in order. Tap one to go back to it. The ↺ button starts a new journey from the current word.
  • Word map — the current word at the centre with related words around it, joined by lines that show the relationship. If a word has several meanings, choose one at the top. Tap a surrounding word to make it the centre of the map; tap the centre word to open it in the article.

On the Mac, Go ▸ Back and Forward (⌘[ and ⌘]) move through the words you have opened.

Looking up a word from another app

Mac

  • Services menu. Highlight a word in any app, then choose Services ▸ Look Up in Graphē Dictionary. To give it a keyboard shortcut, open System Settings ▸ Keyboard ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts ▸ Services ▸ Text.
  • Dock icon. Highlight a word, then click the Graphē Dictionary icon in the Dock. The first time, macOS asks for the Accessibility permission, which lets the app read what you highlighted in the app in front. Switch it on in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Accessibility. Some apps, such as certain browsers, do not share their selection; use the Services menu there.

iPhone and iPad

Copy a word in any app, then open Graphē Dictionary. A short copied word is placed in the search box. iOS asks your permission to paste; to stop the prompt, choose Allow under Settings ▸ Graphē Dictionary ▸ Paste from Other Apps.

Dictionary Data: choosing a size

The app includes the Essential dictionary, which works offline. Larger sizes are optional downloads: open More ▸ Dictionary Data, choose a size and tap Download. A downloaded size replaces the one you had, and an Update button appears when a newer version is available.

SizeAddsApprox. download
EssentialOpen English WordNet and Webster's 1913. Included in the app.167 MB
CompactEveryday Wiktionary words (up to 3 senses each) and FOLDOC computing terms.438 MB
StandardWiktionary phrases and rarer words (up to 4 senses each).562 MB
FullUp to 10 senses per word, with thesaurus links.620 MB
MaximumEverything: all senses, plus inflections, word origins, translations, examples and quotations.1.2 GB

Make sure your device has enough free space before you download; the larger sizes need room for the file plus the copy the app keeps. If you keep dictionary files in a folder of your own, for example in iCloud Drive, you can choose that folder in Dictionary Data instead and download from it.

Spelling: use More ▸ Spelling to show British or American spellings.
