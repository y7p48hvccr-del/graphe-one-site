ScriptureStudy Kids

“Jesus said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come to me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.”
Matthew 19:14

ScriptureStudy Kids is as a bright, peaceful, and imaginative biblical learning environment designed to help young children begin exploring Scripture through shapes, curiosity, storytelling, discovery, and play.

Rather than overwhelming children with information, the platform is designed to create a fresh, happy and welcoming tutorial environment where Scripture becomes accessable, colorful, memorable, and meaningful.

The applications vision is to include bright biblical passages, playful learning activities, puzzles, games, guided exploration, and gentle educational tools intended to encourage both awe and understanding.

ScriptureStudy Kids is also imagined as an introduction to Ancient Hebrew, whose visual and symbolic structure naturally lends itself to childlike discovery, memory learning, and playful language exploration.
