Scrib Privacy Statement

Effective 19 August 2026

The short version: Scrib collects nothing about you. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts of ours. Your documents travel only between your device and cloud storage you own, and we never see them.

What Scrib stores

Scrib works on documents you choose to open. They are stored:

  • On your device — the documents you open, your reading preferences (theme, text size), automatic backups, and a crash-recovery journal.
  • In your own cloud storage — if you open documents from iCloud Drive or OneDrive, they remain in that account, which belongs to you.

Studio Graphē One operates no servers for Scrib. Your documents are never transmitted to us, and we have no ability to read them.

OneDrive sign-in

If you choose to connect OneDrive, you sign in with Microsoft directly, on Microsoft's own sign-in page. Scrib never sees or stores your password. Microsoft issues the app a secure access token, which is kept in your device's protected Keychain and used solely to transfer your documents between your device and your OneDrive. You can revoke this access at any time by signing out in Scrib or from your Microsoft account settings. Microsoft's handling of your account is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What Scrib does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale, sharing, or transfer of any personal data — we hold none to share.
  • No third-party services beyond the cloud provider you yourself connect.

The built-in thesaurus works entirely offline; word lookups never leave your device.

Support correspondence

If you email support@graphe.one, we use your message and address only to reply to you. We do not add you to lists or share your correspondence.

Changes

If this statement ever changes, the current version will always be at this address, with its effective date above.
